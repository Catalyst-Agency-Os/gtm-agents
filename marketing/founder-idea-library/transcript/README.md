# Transcript

Save one transcript here before step 2, named with the date of the call, such as `2026-09-29-podcast.txt`.

A call your team already recorded, such as a founder's podcast, a webinar, a demo or a customer call. Take customer names out first, or let step 2 swap them for roles. Auto captions are fine, and so are speaker labels and filler. The one real problem is audio so bad whole sentences are lost, and that's a recording fix.

Next week, add the new transcript here with the date in its name. The `transcript-to-week` skill reads the newest one.
