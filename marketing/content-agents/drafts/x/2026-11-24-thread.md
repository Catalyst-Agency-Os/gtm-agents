1/ Nobody trusts the CRM because nobody wants to type into it. The CRM isn't dead. The data-entry CRM is.

2/ Before they switched, reps at our first fifty customers said they spent five to six hours a week on CRM admin. That's most of a day. Every week.

3/ A rep's job is to talk to buyers. Every minute they spend typing notes into a form is a minute they're not selling. So of course they skip it.

4/ Most AI in CRMs is a chatbot bolted on the side. Same old database, same empty fields, and a sparkle button that writes you an email.

5/ AI-native means the AI is how the data gets in. If the record is empty, the AI has nothing to work with.

6/ Here's what that looks like. A discovery call lands and the deal updates itself. Who was on the call, the budget they said, the next step and the date. The rep doesn't open anything.

7/ Every field links to where it came from. Click the budget and you see the minute in the call where the buyer said it.

8/ A CRM with fifty features and wrong data is worse than a spreadsheet, because at least everyone knows the spreadsheet is a guess.
