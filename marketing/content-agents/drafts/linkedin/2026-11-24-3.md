Your forecast looks healthy and still misses.

Here's what we found when we looked at our own pipeline last spring.

Thirty-eight percent of the deals marked commit had no buyer activity in the last fourteen days. The rep believed in them. The data said they were dead.

A forecast like that is a mood, not a number.

Now we run on three rules.

1. Fourteen days of buyer silence moves a deal out of the forecast.
2. The buyer's words beat the rep's guess. The review shows the exact line the buyer said, right next to the close date.
3. The review is for decisions, not a roll call. We used to walk all forty deals. Now it's eight to ten.

Our Monday review went from ninety minutes to about twenty-five.

It's hard to argue with a recording.
