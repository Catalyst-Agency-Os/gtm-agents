Last spring I signed off on a forecast that looked healthy and still missed.

When we looked at our own pipeline, thirty-eight percent of the deals marked commit had no buyer activity in the last fourteen days.

The rep believed in them. The data said they were dead.

I'd been that rep, saying it was looking good when I had no idea. I was hoping.

So now fourteen days of silence moves a deal out of the forecast.

The review is for decisions. Do we bring in the CEO on this one? Do we offer a pilot? Do we walk away?

Nobody misses the roll call.
