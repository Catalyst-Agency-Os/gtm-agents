Why does nobody trust the CRM?

Here's the honest answer.

It isn't because reps are lazy. It's because we asked salespeople to be data entry clerks.

I ran sales at two startups before this. At both of them, the CRM was a graveyard of good intentions.

We'd buy it, set it up beautifully, have a kickoff. Six weeks later it was wrong.

Not a little wrong. The close dates were made up. Half the contacts had left their companies.

The fix isn't a better training session.

It's taking the typing away.

The best CRM is the one that's right on Monday morning.
