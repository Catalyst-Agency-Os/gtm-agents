Three problems came up this week. Nobody trusts the CRM, the one RevOps person is buried in cleanup, and the forecast keeps missing.

Picture the Monday pipeline review. Eight smart people, all squinting at a screen going, is this real?

The reps aren't the problem. What they hate is never really the CRM. It's the nagging feeling that they forgot to log something and Monday's going to be awkward. What's going away is asking humans to type that memory in by hand.

The RevOps question comes next. Hire RevOps when your sales leader is spending more than a day a week on reporting and tooling. RevOps people are some of the sharpest people in a company. Don't waste them on cleanup, because cleaning the CRM by hand is like mopping with the tap still running.

Then the forecast. A deal with no buyer activity in fourteen days isn't a deal, it's a hope. We don't delete it. We just stop letting it sit in the forecast. It feels harsh the first time. It's not. It's just honest.

If you only do one thing this week, pull your commit deals and check how many had buyer activity in the last two weeks. That number will tell you how true your forecast is.
