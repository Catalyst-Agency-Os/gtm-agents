# Before step 1

You need four things and a short note, and none of them is code.

1. The Claude desktop app with Cowork on your account. Plans change, so check your own account settings rather than a list written on a date.
2. This folder on your computer, somewhere you can find again. It lives in [GTM Agents on GitHub](https://github.com/Catalyst-Agency-Os/gtm-agents), under marketing. Click Code at the top of the repo, then Download ZIP. If you use git, clone the repo instead.
3. Transcripts of three to five calls your team already recorded, with timestamps. A founder's podcast, a webinar, a demo or a customer call. Auto captions, speaker labels and filler are all fine. Save each one in `transcript/`, named with the date of the call, such as `2026-10-06-podcast.txt`. One call works, and more calls show you which ideas your founder repeats.
4. Three posts that did well, written by the person this content is for. That's usually your founder or the expert on the calls. Paste them into `templates/voice.md` where it says to.

The note is `templates/market.md`, filled in with a line or two per section. Who you sell to, where they talk, who they follow and which channels you publish on.

The build can't start without the transcripts and the three posts, because the kit ships neither.

## One connector

In Claude's settings, under connectors, add Parallel Search, which is free. The research agent uses it to search the web. If your calls are recorded by a tool with a connector in the same list, such as Circleback, add that too, and step 5 can read new calls straight from it.

## Two things that trip people up

Choose this folder itself when Cowork asks which folder to work in. Claude then sees `CLAUDE.md`, the transcripts and the templates together.

Take the transcript of a call with a customer through one pass first. Swap each name for a role and each company for a type, such as "a VP of marketing at a Series B fintech company". The drafts are going on LinkedIn, so nothing in them should identify someone who didn't agree to it.

If your message box no longer shows Chat and Cowork as two options, you have the newer Claude, where the two are one. The steps work the same, and some screens look different.
