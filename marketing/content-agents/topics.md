# Topics, week of 2026-11-24

> Written by the research agent from `market.md` and `ideas.md`. The window is 2026-11-10 to 2026-11-24. The web was searched with Exa standing in for Parallel Search, so 29 pieces were read, from LinkedIn, RevOps and sales newsletters, a RevOps community podcast and trade press. LinkedIn post dates are the dates the search index gives. Reaction and comment counts appear only where the page showed them.

## 1. Pain points

Eight problems, in six themes. The count is how many of the 29 pieces in section 4 raised it. Pieces by vendors, agencies, consultants or news sites count toward the number and never supply the quote.

### The CRM nobody trusts

1. **The CRM goes stale because reps have to type it in, and nobody trusts it.** Raised by 9 pieces.
   > "That was the job. Every open project in our system needed an end date that hadn't already passed, and the contract is where a real one lives."

   Don Frank, Head of Revenue Operations, 2026-11-12. He holds the RevOps seat, and this cleanup was of delivery projects, not deals. https://www.linkedin.com/posts/don-frank-pmp_projectmanagement-professionalservices-activity-7526645923251277827-peMv
2. **The pipeline review turns into a debate about the data instead of the deals.** Raised by 4 pieces.
   > "The forecast call turns into a debate about timing adjustments instead of deals."

   Mike Schorr, a revenue operations executive, 2026-11-13. He doesn't name his company, so the size is unknown. https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mike-schorr-3486812_revops-revenueoperations-forecasting-activity-7527006336283201537-yBVU

### Forecasts that miss

3. **The forecast looks healthy and still misses.** Raised by 5 pieces.
   > "Every sales leader has sat in a forecasting meeting or QBR where a team entered the quarter with 3x pipeline coverage, and still missed their number by a wide margin."

   Paul Cramer, VP Sales, enterprise SaaS, 2026-11-12. He sells enterprise deals, a longer cycle than the startups `market.md` names. https://www.linkedin.com/posts/paulcramer_revenueoperations-salesleadership-cro-activity-7526661977843122176-7Elp

### Switching CRMs

4. **Moving to a new CRM takes months and runs two systems at once.** Raised by 5 pieces. Every one of them is by a vendor, an agency, a consultant or a news site, so this pain carries no quote. Nobody in the buyer seats raised it in the window.

### RevOps stretched thin

5. **RevOps is a team of one, with too much still run by hand.** Raised by 5 pieces.
   > "I run RevOps at Ketch without a systems team."

   Steven Musche, VP of Revenue Operations at Ketch, 2026-11-17. https://www.linkedin.com/posts/stevenmusche_i-run-revops-at-ketch-without-a-systems-team-activity-7528532619949604864-KdRB

### Founder-led sales

6. **The founder's selling lives in their head, and the first AE can't copy it.** Raised by 5 pieces.
   > "In most startups (with a few exceptions), technology and code is the easy part. People, positioning, framing, and the market are always the hardest parts."

   Kushal Byatnal, CEO of Extend, 2026-11-16. He's a founder, and his post argues for selling early, a step before the handoff the other pieces describe. https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kushal-byatnal-92a33085_my-1-piece-of-advice-for-founders-in-yc-activity-7528164779459514368-8Oyl

### AI in sales

7. **Outbound, run by AI or by an agency, isn't booking real meetings.** Raised by 5 pieces.
   > "I wasted $30k on agencies trying to make outbound work for us. They failed twice. No ROI. So I fired them."

   David Walsh, founder of Limelight, 2026-11-10. https://www.linkedin.com/posts/david-walsh-limelight_i-wasted-30k-on-agencies-trying-to-make-activity-7525943133239697408-bTrq
8. **AI on top of bad CRM data makes confident mistakes.** Raised by 4 pieces.
   > "Like you need somebody that understands the data and understands how all the plumbing works and how the connections work."

   Mitch Larson, Head of Revenue Operations at RightRev, a team of one, on the RevOpsAF podcast, 2026-11-21. https://exa.ai/library/podcast/3twxldckt9l/episode/7hj1bcf7rhz
