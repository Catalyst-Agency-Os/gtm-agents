# Ideas

> The library of everything the person on your calls says that's worth publishing. Step 1 and the content-research skill add to it from every call. Each idea keeps the call and the minute it was said, so any piece of content drawn from it can be checked in seconds.

## How each entry reads

- **The idea, in their own words.** belief, story, number or quote · call name [12:40], with a second source when it came up again · used in, a short name such as linkedin/2026-11-24-1, left blank until a draft uses it

## Said on more than one call

An idea moves here once a second call says it too. These are the ones the speaker believes most, so they're the first ones to plan from.

## Themes

Step 1 adds a heading for each theme as ideas arrive, and files every idea under one.
